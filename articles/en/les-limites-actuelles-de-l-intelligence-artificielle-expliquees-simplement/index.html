The current limitations of artificial intelligence explained simply

Understand the main limitations of artificial intelligence and find out why its responses must be verified before any significant use. .

Cover image Article

Artificial intelligence has become a useful tool for writing, translate, analyze information and create content. However, its results are not always accurate and its functioning has several important limits.

Understanding these limits makes it possible to make better use of AI tools, avoid some errors and keep a critical look at the answers obtained.

IA can produce false information

One of the main limitations of AI is its ability to generate a response which seems convincing but contains an error. The text may be well written, organized and presented with confidence while being incorrect.

This phenomenon is sometimes called hallucination. It may concern a date, a name, statistic, source or explanation of an event. The AI does not always clearly warn the user when it is Not sure of his answer.

For this reason, check important information with sources reliable before publishing or using them.

Integrated image in the body

IA does not understand as a human being

An AI can analyze words, images and relationships between different information. It can also produce a text that looks like a human conversation.

This does not mean that she understands the world in the same way as a No one. She does not automatically have personal experience, a human conscience or judgment.

So she can misinterpret an ambiguous sentence, a joke, an emotion or a situation that requires common sense.

Quality depends on data

The systems of the AIA learn from large amounts of data. If these data are incomplete, old, incorrect or unbalanced, results may be limited.

This idea is often summed up by the expression "garbage in, garbage out": when the information used is poor, the result may also of being.

An AI cannot automatically distinguish information reliable information in its data.

Bias can influence results

A bias appears when a system produces unbalanced results or unfavourable to certain persons, groups or groups situations.

The biases can come from the data, the way the model was or how the results are interpreted. They can influence an automated recommendation, classification or decision.

To be noted: an automatic response shall be considered with attention when dealing with a person, group or important decision.

IA may lack context

A response depends heavily on the information provided in the request. If the context is incomplete, the AI can propose a solution that does not doesn't really fit your situation.

For example, a general recommendation for a company, a journey or learning method may become inappropriate if the budget, location, level or constraints are not specified.

The clearer your request, the more information the tool has for producing an appropriate response. Even in this case, the result must be assessed by the user.

IA does not replace human expertise

An AI can help understand a topic or prepare a first version a document. It must not, however, always replace a doctor, lawyer, accountant, teacher or other professional.

In sensitive areas such as health, law, finance or safety, an error can have important consequences. The opinion of a qualified person therefore remains necessary.

Personal data pose a risk

To get a personalized answer, some users copy private conversations, professional documents or information concerning other persons.

This practice can expose sensitive data. Avoid sharing passwords, bank information, identity documents or confidential files without having checked the rules of the service.

How to use AI with caution

  1. Check important facts with several reliable sources.
  2. Do not share personal or confidential data.
  3. Ask for explanations when the answer seems imprecise.
  4. Use AI as an aid and not as an absolute authority.
  5. Consult a professional for sensitive decisions.

Conclusion

Artificial intelligence can be very useful, but it does not guarantee not always accurate, complete or adapted results. She can be wrong, lack of context, reproduce biases or produce a response that seems reliable without actually being.

The best way to use it is to give it instructions protect its data and verify results. Critical mind remains indispensable to enjoy the benefits of AI without dependent All of his answers.
