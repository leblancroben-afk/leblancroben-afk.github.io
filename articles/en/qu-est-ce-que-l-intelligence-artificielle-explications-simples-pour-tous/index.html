What is artificial intelligence? Simple explanations for all

A clear presentation of basic concepts: AI, automatic learning, networks of neurons and concrete examples of everyday life.

Cover image Article

Artificial intelligence, often called AI, refers to a whole technologies capable of performing certain tasks that require usually human intelligence. It may in particular analyse information, recognize images, understand text, translate a language or propose recommendations.

The purpose of the AI is not necessarily to create a conscious machine or able to think like a person. In most cases, It is mainly used to automate tasks, identify trends and to help users make better decisions.

How does artificial intelligence work?

An AI system usually works from data, a) the algorithms and model. The data can be text, images, sounds, numbers or videos. The algorithm analyses this information in order to produce a result.

Integrated image in the body

What is machine learning?

Machine learning, or machine learning, is the main branches of artificial intelligence. For manually program all the rules, we provide the system many examples so that he can discover regularities.

For example, to learn how to recognize a cat in a photo, a model can receive several thousand identified images as representing a cat or other animal. He then analyses the characteristics of the images and uses what he learned to classify new photos.

The role of neuron networks

Neuron networks are inspired computer models, in a simplified way, the functioning of the human brain. They have several treatment layers capable of analysis complex information.

Deep neuron networks, also known as deep learning, are particularly used for recognize images, understand speech, translate texts and generate content.

Examples in everyday life

Even without knowing it, many people already use AI Every day. It is integrated into many applications and Digital services.

Second integrated image
  • Recommendations: video platforms, content according to your interests.
  • Virtual Assistants: they can understand some voice commands and answer simple questions.
  • Automatic translation: IA helps to translate quickly sentences and documents.
  • Email filtering: it can identify unwanted or potentially dangerous messages.
  • Image recognition: it can detect objects, faces or certain visual characteristics.

What are the limits of AI?

The AI is powerful, but it is not perfect. It depends on the quality of the data used for training. If these data are incomplete or biased, the results can also be incorrect or unjust.

An AI can also produce an answer that seems convincing But it's not true. It is therefore important to check the important information and to consider AI as an aid tool, and not as an infallible source.

How to use AI responsibly?

  • Check the important information provided by an AI.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal data.
  • Compare results with reliable sources.
  • Keep human control over important decisions.

Conclusion

Artificial intelligence brings together capable technologies analyse data and perform different tasks automatically. It is already present in many aspects but its use requires caution, and special attention to protection data.
