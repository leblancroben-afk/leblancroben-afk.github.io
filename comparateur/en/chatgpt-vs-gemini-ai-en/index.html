ChatGPT vs Gemini

ChatGPT and Gemini are two versatile AI assistants able to write, summarize, analyze documents, generate images and answer complex questions. ChatGPT stands out for its creative tools, custom GPT and writing experience, while Gemini benefits from its integration with Google, multi-modal capabilities and its connection to the Workspace ecosystem.

Strengths & limitations
ChatGPT — Strengths
  • Very good quality of writing and reformulation
  • Clear and versatile interface
  • Creating Custom TPMs
  • Advanced functions for projects and workspaces
  • Good file and document analysis
  • Integrated tools for in-depth search, images and coding
  • Wide ecosystem of applications and integrations
Gemini — Strengths
  • Native integration with Gmail, Google Docs, Google Drive and other Google services
  • Good understanding of images, documents and other multimedia formats
  • Practical access to ecosystem information Google
  • Long context available according to model and package
  • Integration with NotebookLM and some Google AI services
  • Google storage One included with some paid packages
  • Free generous version for current use
ChatGPT — Limitations
  • The limits of use depend on the package and the model selected
  • Some advanced features are reserved for paid subscriptions
  • Answers may contain errors or invented information
  • Google integrations are less native than those of Gemini
  • Prices and features may vary by region
Gemini — Limitations
  • Message limits and access to advanced models can change regularly
  • Some integrations Google requires a paid package
  • The quality of responses may vary depending on the task and model used
  • Some advanced functions may be limited to some countries
  • The continuity between the different functions of the Google ecosystem may be unclear
  • Results should be verified for important topics

🏆 ChatGPT recommended

ChatGPT wins this comparison for the general versatility, writing, customization and variety of its tools. It is a particularly suitable choice for content creators, developers, students and professionals who want to use a flexible AI assistant. Gemini, however, remains the best choice for people who work mainly with Gmail, Google Docs, Google Drive and other Google services. Its integration with Google Workspace and storage space included in certain packages can represent real added value.
