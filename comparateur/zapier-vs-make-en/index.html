Zapier vs Make

Zapier and Make compete at the top of codeless automation. While Make impresses with its visual design and aggressive pricing, Zapier takes precedence over operational efficiency thanks to its unrivalled catalog of over 8,000 applications, its disconcerting simplicity and advanced AI features via Copilot. This is the ultimate choice for teams who want to deploy reliable flows at lightning speed.

Strengths & limitations
Zapier — Strengths
  • Immediate grip and step-by-step configuration accessible to all
  • More than 8,000 integrated applications (the largest market catalogue)
  • Assistant Zapier Copilot high performance to generate natural language streams
  • Legendary stability of connections and ultra-simple error management
Make — Strengths
  • Extremely competitive transaction rate for large data volumes
  • Very powerful visual canvas for mapping complex scenarios
  • Native and advanced loop (Iterators) and cluster (Aggregators) management
  • Free generous version including multi-step and routers
Zapier — Limitations
  • Cost climbs fast if you have millions of tasks to perform
  • Very limited free version (no webhooks, 2 steps maximum)
  • Less suitable for highly branched and visual flow architectures
Make — Limitations
  • Shorter learning curve (technical concepts of variables to master)
  • Each enabled module (even a filter or tool) consumes an operation, which quickly clears the package
  • Fewer niche or local applications indexed natively than its competitor
Who should choose what?
Choose
Zapier

Choose Zapier if you want your marketing, commercial or HR teams to be autonomous in their automations without asking developers. This is the essential tool if you are using niche software or are betting on AI (Copilot) to design and maintain your flow without friction within minutes.

Choose
Make

Choose Make if you have technical profiles (Ops, Devs) in-house, scenarios requiring massive manipulation of complex databases (interlocked loops), and an imperative need to reduce costs on industrial execution volumes.

🏆 Zapier recommended

Although Make is positioned as a serious economic challenger thanks to its visual interface and attractive volume prices, Zapier retains its crown as the undisputed leader of the iPaaS. Its strength lies in its ecosystem: with more than 8,000 connected applications and an integration of AI (Zapier Copilot) that removes any technical barriers, it allows to create flows in seconds where Make requires real logical reflection. For scalability, speed of execution of teams and assurance of finding any software on the market, Zapier remains the great winner of this duel.

FAQ
Zapier uses a linear and textual automation structure ("If this happens in application A, then do this in application B"). It is a logic modelled on natural language. Make uses a 2D canvas where you connect bubbles (modules) between them, which requires understanding raw data manipulation, JSON structures and variable logic.
Zapier only bills successful "actions" (the steps that modify or send data). Triggers and filters are free. On the other hand, Make bills each module interaction: a trigger that checks if there is any new, a filter that blocks a data or an internal tool all consume operations, which can empty your credits faster than expected.
Yes, both platforms integrate AI in 2026. Zapier offers "Zapier Copilot", an extremely mature conversational assistant who can design an entire stream from a simple sentence in French. Make integrates AI assistants to help you generate code or structure your modules on its visual canvas.
