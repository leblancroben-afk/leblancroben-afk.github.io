Agent IA

Artificial intelligence system capable of performing tasks independently, making decisions and interacting with its environment without constant human intervention.

🎯 Why it matters
Understanding this concept allows us to anticipate the future of work where AI no longer merely responds, but acts in our place. It is the transition from a passive tool to a proactive collaborator able to manage complex projects from end to end. Mastering this concept helps to design complete workflow automations rather than single tasks.
💡 Concrete example
AutoGPT is an IA agent that can browse the internet, write code and perform automatically chained tasks.
⚙️ In practice
An AI agent works with a feedback loop: it perceives its environment, plans actions, uses external tools (web search, databases, scripts) and evaluates its own results to adjust. To deploy it, frameworks such as LangChain or CrewAI are used to define specific roles and connect the agent to APIs. The key to its success is the clarity of the initial instructions and the management of its limits to prevent it from closing indefinitely.
Common mistake: Consolidate an AI agent with a simple chatbot. While a chatbot waits for instructions at each stage, the agent receives a global goal and decides on the next steps.
