Fine tuning

Additional training process for a pre-existing AI model on specific data to specialize in a specific area.

🎯 Why it matters
This method makes it possible to transform a generalist AI into a highly specialized expert for your business or business. It avoids having to retrain a model from the beginning, which would cost millions of dollars and require enormous resources.
💡 Concrete example
A law firm can terminate a LLM on thousands of contracts to create a specialized legal assistant.
⚙️ In practice
The fine tuning consists of adjusting the internal weights of an already trained model by submitting it to a high quality, targeted data set. Lightweight techniques such as LoRA are often used to generate only a small fraction of the parameters, reducing memory and computing power requirements. This is the ideal approach to impose a very specific tone, comply with strict safety instructions or master complex technical jargon.
Common mistake: Consolidate the fine tuning, which changes the behavior and style of the model, with the RAG, which simply gives it access to external documents to answer a question.
