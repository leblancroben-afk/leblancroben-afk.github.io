🌱 Débutant

Hallucination

A phenomenon where an AI model generates false, invented or incorrect information with apparent confidence. One of the major challenges of the current LMLs.

🎯 Why it matters
Understanding this phenomenon is essential to avoid blind confidence in AI responses. This encourages a critical mindset and systematic verification of critical information, thus avoiding the spread of errors in work or studies. It is the key to using artificial intelligence as a tool of assistance and not as a source of absolute truth.
💡 Concrete example
Asking ChatGPT to quote sources can lead to non-existent URLs invented from all parts.
⚙️ In practice
Technically, hallucinations stem from the fact that language models predict the most likely word statistically, without a real understanding of the facts. To limit this risk, one can use prompt engineering techniques by ordering AI to answer 'I don't know' if it lacks information. The integration of external databases (RAG) and the decrease in the 'temperature' of the model also reduce creativity and thus the likelihood of inventions.
Common mistake: Hallucination is often confused with an intentional lie. AI has no conscience or intention to deceive; It simply assembles words in a statistically consistent manner, without any notion of truth.
❓ Frequently asked questions
AI models are designed to generate fluid and convincing texts. Not aware of the truth, they calculate probabilities of words, which gives their mistakes the same assertive and natural tone as their exact answers.
Prefer specific instructions and ask AI to quote its sources or break down its reasoning. Above all, always cross critical data (figures, dates, laws) with reliable external sources before using them.
