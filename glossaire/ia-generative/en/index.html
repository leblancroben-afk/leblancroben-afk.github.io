🌱 Débutant

General

Artificial intelligence branch capable of creating new content — text, images, music, video, code — from a request in natural language.

🎯 Why it matters
This technology transforms our way of working and designing projects by acting as a collaborator available at any time. It allows everyone to overcome the white page syndrome and to realize an idea, whether to write a text or to design a visual, without prior technical skills.
💡 Concrete example
Jasper generates articles, Midjourney creates images, Suno composes music — all are general AI.
⚙️ In practice
Under the hood, these systems rely on deep neuron networks trained on huge databases to identify structures and patterns. When asked (a 'prompt'), the AI does not think, but statistically calculates the most likely sequence of words or pixels. To use it effectively, it should be provided with a precise context, a clear role and examples of the expected result.
Common mistake: A common mistake is to believe that the general AI really understands what it produces or always tells the truth. In reality, it seeks statistical consistency, which can lead it to invent facts with misleading assurance: this is what is called hallucinations.
❓ Frequently asked questions
AI does not create anything from nothing; She combines and reorganizes patterns learned during her training. Although the final result may seem new, it is a sophisticated imitation rather than a true artistic or intellectual inspiration.
In most countries, copyright only protects creations from human originality. Content generated entirely by a machine generally falls into the public domain, although the use of complex queries may sometimes open a legal debate.
