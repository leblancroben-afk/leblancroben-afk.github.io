Prompt engineering

Art and science to formulate optimal instructions for obtaining the best results of an AI model. High demand in the labour market.

🎯 Why it matters
Mastering this skill makes it possible to obtain infinitely more accurate and useful responses from AI, thus avoiding wasting time with generic results. This is the key to transforming an entertainment tool into a real daily productivity assistant.
💡 Concrete example
Adding "Step by Step" to a prompt significantly improves complex reasoning responses.
⚙️ In practice
In practice, this involves structuring requests by providing a clear role, a precise context, constraints and the expected output format. You can also use the "few-shot prompting" by giving two or three examples of desired results before asking the final question. This approach guides the model towards the optimal path of reasoning to solve complex tasks.
Common mistake: Think it is a frozen magic formula while it is an iterative process of testing and adjusting according to the model used.
