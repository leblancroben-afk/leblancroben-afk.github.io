What is Midjourney and how to create images with this AI tool?

Short answer

Midjourney is an AI tool that generates images from text, reference image or combination of both, via its website or Discord.

What is Midjourney?

Midjourney is an artificial intelligence tool that creates images from a written description called prompt. You can use it on the Midjourney site or in Discord, according to your preference.

  • It generates original visuals from text.
  • He also accepts images as a source of inspiration.
  • It offers options to change style, format and some details.
  • It is particularly used for visual creation, marketing and creative concepts.

How to write a good quick Midjourney?

A good prompt clearly indicates what you want to see, in what setting, with what atmosphere and visual style. Midjourney recommends specifying the environment, lighting, colors and, if necessary, image format:

photo of a cup of coffee, natural light soft, minimalist background, warm colors, format 16:9

  • Avoid phrases too vague as "a beautiful image".
  • Test several formulations rather than one version.

Is Midjourney paying?

Midjourney works with a subscription; it is not designed as a completely free tool. The choice of the plan determines in particular the generation time, the features available and the conditions of commercial use. Always check the current rates and conditions on the official website before you subscribe.

  • Choose a plan according to your creation volume.
  • Check the rights to use the generated images.
  • Avoid generating misleading, illegal or infringing content.
