What AI tools are free without limit?

Short answer

Few AI tools are really free without limit. The solutions installed on your computer, such as Ollama or LM Studio, are the best approach, provided your machine is quite powerful

Detailed answer

There are several artificial intelligence tools available free of charge. However, it is necessary to distinguish entirely free tools from those that offer a free version with restrictions.

Online platforms usually impose limits, even when they display a free formula. Locally executed software avoids these quotas, but requires a sufficiently powerful computer and storage space.

Key takeaways

  • Free does not always mean unlimited.
  • Always check the terms of use before committing.
