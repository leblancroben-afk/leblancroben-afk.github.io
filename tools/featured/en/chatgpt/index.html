What is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is the conversational agent developed by OpenAI that has democratized artificial generative intelligence. Designed to understand and generate natural language, it adapts to an infinity of professional and personal use cases.

Whether you need to write a professional email, debug a programming script, synthesize a long report or brainstorm marketing ideas, the tool runs in seconds. It now integrates advanced reasoning models (O series) to solve complex logical problems.

In addition to the text, ChatGPT is multimodal: it hears your voice, analyzes your images and generates unique visuals through the integration of DALL-E. Its clean interface hides a formidable productivity tool that integrates into the daily lives of millions of professionals.
