About Claude

Developed by Anthropic, Claude established himself as ChatGPT's main rival by relying on an ethical, secure and deeply effective AI. The Claude 3 and 3.5 model family shines especially with its superior reasoning abilities.

One of Claude's greatest assets is his mastery of language. His texts are more nuanced, less stereotyped and adapt perfectly to complex editorial charters. For developers, its programming skills compete with the best tools on the market.

The interface is distinguished by the introduction of "Artifacts", which transform the simple cat into a co-creation environment. You can see a website or mini-game build before your eyes and interact with the result in real time, without leaving the application.
