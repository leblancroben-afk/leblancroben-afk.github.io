What is ElevenLabs?

EvenLabs is a complete AI audio platform, ranging from voice synthesis to the creation of conversational agents. It serves both individual creators and large companies.

The platform offers expressive voice models, instant or professional vocal cloning, automatic dubbing in 90+ languages, and high-precision transcription. It also includes the generation of music and sound effects.

For developers, ElevenLabs presents REST APIs and Python/TypeScript SDKs to integrate voice into applications. Companies can deploy in private cloud with compliance options (HIPAA, SSO, zero retention).
