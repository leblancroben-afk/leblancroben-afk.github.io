What is Gemini?

Developed by Google, Gemini is a leading artificial intelligence assistant that replaces the old Bard model. Designed from the outset to be multimodal, it is able to seamlessly process and combine different types of information, including text, code, images, audio and video. This versatility makes it a tool of choice for professionals and individuals seeking to automate daily tasks or stimulate their creativity.

One of Gemini's greatest assets is its native integration with the Google Workspace ecosystem. You can use it to scan documents stored on Google Drive, write drafts in Gmail or extract data from Google Docs. The Advanced version, accessible via the Google One AI Premium subscription, offers enhanced performance thanks to the Gemini Pro model and an exceptionally wide context window to process large volumes of data.
