About Writesonic

Writesonic is a comprehensive IA platform combining writing long articles, SEO optimization and GEO visibility tracking (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews).

The free plan offers 10K words/month with GPT-3.5. Standard plans ($79/mo per year), Basic ($199/mo) and Growth ($399/mo) offer 15-50 items/month, SEO audits and GEO tracking on several IA platforms.

Writesonic includes Chatsonic (chatbot with web access), an image generator, and WordPress integrations, Shopify, Webflow, Zapier and Surfer SEO. Ideal for SEO editors and agencies.
