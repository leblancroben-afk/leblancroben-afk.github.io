About Fathom

Fathom is an IA notetaker designed for Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams meetings. It captures the call, produces a transcript, a summary and actions to follow, and then makes these elements searchable and shareable.

The tool helps teams save time after each meeting: instant summaries, clips, highlights, playlists, research into the history of exchanges and follow-up of decisions. He also proposes Ask Fathom to question meetings that have passed in natural language.

Fathom includes Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Conception and Asana. Its API and webhooks allow to send transcripts, summaries and actions to other business tools.

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Free plan with unlimited IA meetings, transcripts and summaries
  • Transcription and native summaries in French, English, Spanish, German, Italian, Dutch and Portuguese
  • Instant summaries, actions to follow, clips, highlights and research in meetings
  • Compatible Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams
  • API, webhooks, SDK and MCP integration available, including free
  • CRM Integrations and Collaboration: HubSpot, Salesforce, Slack, Conception and Asana

Limitations

  • Advanced team functions, including Trackers, Scorecards, Deal View and CRM synchronization, require a paid plan
  • The transcription and native summaries cover 7 languages; other languages go mainly through translation via Ask Fathom
  • Bot-free capture is still in beta on Mac
  • Rates for paid offers vary according to monthly or annual billing

Use ideas

  • Generate structured reporting after a client call
  • Extract actions and leaders
  • Create a team meeting summary to share on Slack
  • Find a decision or commitment mentioned in a previous meeting
  • Preparing for commercial monitoring from an exchange with a prospect
