About FineVoice AI

FineVoice is an audio all-in-one platform that transforms text into natural voices, creates voiceovers, clones or edits voices, generates sound effects and produces background music.

The platform offers over 2,700 IA voices in more than 154 languages and accents. Users can adjust the tone, speed, emotion and style to produce narratives, advertisements, e-learning content, podcasts, videos and animated characters.

FineVoice also includes audio-text transcription, voice translation, audio enhancement, the creation of speaking avatars and the generation of sound effects from text, image or video. A REST API with SDK Python and JavaScript is available to integrate these functions into applications.

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Over 2,700 IA voices in more than 154 languages and accents
  • Generating expressive off voice with tone, speed and emotion settings
  • Voice cloning from audio sample about 30 seconds
  • Creating personalized voices from a quick text
  • Generation of rights-free sound effects from text, image or video
  • Background music generation and audio-text transcription
  • REST API with SDK Python and JavaScript
  • Export MP3 or WAV compatible with CapCut, Premiere Pro and other publishers

Limitations

  • The free plan quota is limited to 1,000 TTS characters per day
  • Unused appropriations and minutes shall not be carried over to the following month
  • Voice cloning must respect consent, copyright and usage rules
  • Some advanced features require a paid subscription
