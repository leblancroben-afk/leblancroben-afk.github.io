About iZotope RX

iZotope RX is the standard music and audiovisual industry software dedicated to restoration and sound correction. It is used by both sound engineers and experienced music producers and podcasters. Thanks to the progressive integration of artificial intelligence, RX facilitates the elimination of unwanted noises, clicks, breath or annoying resonances to restore the original clarity of voices and instruments.

Key features

Denoise & De-reverb

Isolate the spoken or sung voice and eliminates reverberation and background noise.

Repair Assistant

Analyze audio to automatically detect defects and propose suitable settings.

Separation of sources

Separates and isolates individual elements such as voice, battery or bass on a mixed file.
