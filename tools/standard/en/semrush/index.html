About Semrush

Semrush is a digital marketing platform that allows to analyze and improve the online visibility of a site. It brings together key word search, position tracking, technical audit, backlink analysis and competition study tools.

The platform also helps create SEO-optimized content through subject ideas, editorial recommendations and IA-assisted optimization functions. It also allows to track the visibility of a brand in responses generated by ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and other IA engines.

Semrush also offers solutions for pay advertising, local SEO, social networks and competitive analysis. Its API allows to integrate keywords, traffic and IA visibility data into dashboards or internal tools.

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Full suite for SEO, content, advertising and social networks
  • Search for keywords, position tracking and backlink analysis
  • Technical site audit and optimization recommendations
  • Competitive analysis and site traffic study
  • Follow-up of brand visibility in IA responses
  • Free account without credit card and 7 day trial on most toolkits

Limitations

  • The free account is limited to 1 demonstration project and 10 reports per day
  • Advanced features require a paid subscription
  • The interface can be complex for beginners
  • The price depends on the toolkit(s) chosen, which can rapidly increase the budget

Use ideas

  • Find keywords with high potential for a blog article
  • Analyze backlinks and SEO strategies of a competitor
  • Audit a site to identify technical errors
  • Follow the evolution of Google's site positions
  • Identify subjects that generate traffic in a niche
