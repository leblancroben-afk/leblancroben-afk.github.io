About SocialEcho

SocialEcho is a SaaS IA platform designed to centralize social media management. It allows you to create content, publish it on multiple platforms, respond to comments and messages, monitor mentions, keywords and competitors, and analyze results.

The tool supports 11 platforms: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, TikTok Shop, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Telegram, Pinterest, Threads and VK. Accounts are allowed individually via official APIs, without password storage.

SocialEcho also offers AI automations and an agent capable of creating, publishing, responding and reporting. Advanced features, including AI responses and competitive monitoring, are reserved for paid offers.

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • IA creation of videos, images and legends adapted to platforms

Limitations

  • Free offer limited to one social account

  • iOS app is still in development
