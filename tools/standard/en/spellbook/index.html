About Spellbook

Spellbook is an artificial intelligence platform for lawyers, internal legal teams and professionals working with contracts. It allows you to write entire clauses or contracts, identify risks and missing clauses, and then propose changes in the form of changes tracking in Microsoft Word.

The tool also incorporates a question-and-answer function on the open contract, with answers supported by quotations from the passages concerned. Its "Compare to Market" function compares a clause or contract with market data from thousands of similar agreements.

Spellbook works in Microsoft Word, Google Docs and on the web. It also proposes "Associate", an agent capable of processing multi-document projects, such as preparing a contract file from a sheet term or reviewing a data room.

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Complete contractual review with risk detection, missing clauses and redlining suggestions.
  • Writing clauses and contracts from a simple instruction.
  • Questions on the open document with quotations to the relevant clauses.
  • Comparison with market data from thousands of similar contracts.
  • Native integration with Microsoft Word and Google Docs.
  • Associate agent for multi-document tasks and complex legal workflows.

Limitations

  • Pricing only on quotation, without public grid.
  • Free trial limited to 7 days.
  • Designed primarily for legal professionals and contract teams.
  • The quality of the results may depend on the legal context, jurisdiction and language used.
