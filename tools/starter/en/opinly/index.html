About Opinly

Opinly is a SEO growth platform that transforms competitive information into automated actions. It allows to audit a site, identify topics and keywords to be used, generate optimized content and program it for publication.

The tool also monitors competitors: keywords, backlinks, traffic trends, destination pages and organic or pay performance. These data are used to prioritize content opportunities and to monitor the evolution of visibility in research results.

Opinly integrates with common CMS and frameworks, including WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Next.js, Nuxt and Svelte. It also proposes a backlink exchange function driven by IA to develop the authority of a site.

Strengths and limitations

Strengths

  • Technical SEO audit and organic visibility monitoring.
  • SEO optimized content generation and programming.
  • Keyword tracking, backlinks and competitor performance.
  • Analysis of competing destination pages and traffic trends.
  • Integrations with WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Next.js, Nuxt and Svelte.
  • Automated backlink exchange function.

Limitations

  • Relatively high fee for independents, starting at $24 per month in annual billing.
  • Advanced features reserved for Business and Agency offers.
  • Interface and public information mainly in English.
  • Free trial not publicly confirmed on the pages consulted.
