About SE Ranking

SE Ranking is a SEO platform designed to help companies, independents and agencies improve their visibility on Google and search engines. It brings together more than 30 tools for keyword tracking, technical site audit, backlink analysis, competitive research, local SEO and reporting.

The platform allows you to track key word positions in different regions and languages, to identify technical problems of a site, to analyze incoming link profiles and to compare its performance with that of competitors. It also integrates visibility-related functions into AI searches and offers API access to integrate data into dashboards or external applications.

SE Ranking offers a free trial of 14 days, without credit card, including up to 10 projects, 750 keywords followed per day, 20 IA prompts per day and 100,000 API credits.

Limitations
